Dataset Format Guidelines

Your CSV must contain two fundamental columns. Flexible header variations are automatically recognized:

  • datetime: Timestamps (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS or standard date/time strings).
  • consumption_kwh: Numerical energy meter reading in kWh (or usage, kwh, units).
  • Frequency: Hourly records (at least 24 contiguous readings are recommended).
